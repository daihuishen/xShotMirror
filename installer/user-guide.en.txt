xShot Mirror 1.0.0 - User Guide

Mirror your iPhone screen to a Windows computer. Supports windowed viewing, full screen, and PIN pairing.
Mirroring audio plays through the current default Windows output device. Recording and DRM-protected video are not supported.

Requirements
Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11 on a 64-bit Intel or AMD computer.
Connect your computer and iPhone to the same local network. Wired and Wi-Fi devices must be able to communicate; isolated guest networks will not work.
.NET 10 and the required audio/video runtime are included. MSYS2 and developer tools are not needed.
Apple Bonjour must be installed separately, with Bonjour Service running.
Apple's official download information: https://support.apple.com/106380
Apple's page lists older Windows versions. Verify that Bonjour works on your target Windows version.
This installer does not include, modify, or uninstall Apple Bonjour.

Getting started
1. Keep "Create a desktop shortcut" and "Allow iPhone connections on the same local network" selected during installation.
2. Open xShot Mirror from your desktop or Start menu, then click "Start receiving".
3. On your iPhone, open Control Center > Screen Mirroring and select xShot Mirror.
4. Enter the four-digit PIN shown on your computer.
5. Click "Full screen" to enlarge the picture. Press Esc or F11 to return to the window.

If the receiver is not listed, check Bonjour Service, your local network, and your router's device isolation settings.
Third-party firewalls must also allow Bonjour's UDP port 5353 and local-subnet connections to this application's receiver\uxplay.exe.
You do not need to disable your firewall or configure router port forwarding.

Uninstalling
Uninstall xShot Mirror from Windows Settings > Apps.
Uninstalling removes the firewall rules created by this installer. Cached files remain in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\xShotMirror and can be deleted manually if needed.
